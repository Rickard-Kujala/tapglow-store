## Order Processing

Orders are fulfilled through our fulfillment partner and are typically processed within 1–3 business days of purchase. Processing times may occasionally be longer during high-demand periods or holidays.

## Tracking

Once your order has shipped, we'll email you tracking information where available, so you can follow your package's progress.

## Customs, Duties & Import Taxes

Depending on your country, your order may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or other fees charged by local authorities. These charges are not included in your order total and are the customer's responsibility. We recommend checking with your local customs office if you're unsure what to expect.

## Delivery Estimates

Any delivery estimates provided at checkout or in order confirmation emails are approximate and not guaranteed. We are not responsible for delays caused by the carrier, customs, or other factors beyond our control.
