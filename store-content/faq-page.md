## Frequently asked questions

**How long does shipping take?**
Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. Delivery time after that depends on your destination. You'll see an estimated delivery window at checkout, and full tracking details once your order ships.

**Where do you ship?**
We ship worldwide.

**Can I track my order?**
Yes. As soon as your order ships, we'll email you tracking information so you can follow it the whole way.

**Can I return my order?**
Yes. You can request a return within 14 days of delivery, as long as the product is unused and in its original condition. See our Return & Refund Policy for full details.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Google Pay, all processed securely through Shopify Payments.

**Do I need to pay customs fees?**
Depending on your country, customs duties or import taxes may apply. These are set by your local authorities and are the customer's responsibility, not included in your order total.

**My order arrived damaged.**
We're sorry to hear that. Email us at support@tapglow.se with your order number and a photo of the damage, and we'll sort out a replacement or refund.

**I entered the wrong shipping address.**
Contact us at support@tapglow.se as soon as possible. If your order hasn't shipped yet, we'll do our best to update it — once it's with our fulfillment partner, we may not be able to change the address.

**How do I contact TapGlow?**
Email us at support@tapglow.se and we'll get back to you, usually within 24–48 business hours.
