We want you to be happy with your TapGlow order. If something isn't right, here's how returns and refunds work.

## Return Window

You may request a return within 14 days of the delivery date.

## Condition of Returned Items

To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused, in its original condition, and in its original packaging.

## Exceptions

The following items are not eligible for return:

- Personalized or custom-made products
- Products that have been damaged due to customer misuse after delivery

## How to Request a Return

Email us at support@tapglow.se with your order number and the reason for your return. We'll confirm your eligibility and provide instructions, including the return address:

## Return Shipping

Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, unless the item arrived defective or damaged, or return shipping is required to be covered by law in your jurisdiction. Original shipping fees are non-refundable.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method, typically within a few business days of inspection. Please note it may take additional time for your bank or card provider to process and post the refund.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrived damaged or defective, contact us at support@tapglow.se with photos and your order number, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund at no additional cost to you.
